## Limitations

- The log does not record the battery current ("Ibat"). Thus, the rotor inertia of the governor model has a larger uncertainty. The set of models also has 0.7 and 1.4 times the rotor time constant, and a larger governor gain is a check.
- The test flights do not show the rotor time constant of the model. Thus, the set of models also has 1.4 times this time constant.
- For "kv", the uncertainty of the governor model is 311.7 to 336.5 rpm/V, and the set of models has 315.2 to 337.2 rpm/V. Thus, the ranges of the results are possibly too small.
- The RPM signal of the governor model has no vibration. Thus, a larger gain or a filter with a higher cutoff can give more fast throttle changes in flight than in the model.
- At a headspeed that the flights do not use, the governor model calculates the load from the flights at the other headspeeds.
- The battery voltage of the governor model does not increase again after a load decrease. Thus, in the first seconds of a flight, the battery voltage of the model can be too low.
- The model calculates the effect of a change. A flight with the change shows the effect on the helicopter.
